I led the inaugural Social Sciences track at FLI SCI (First-Generation/Low-Income Scientists), a nonprofit dedicated to expanding research opportunities for first-generation and limited-access high school students.

My goal for this course was to not treat it as a conventional college course. Instead, I approached curriculum design by putting myself in my students' shoes and reflecting on what I would have needed when I was in a similar position. We started with something as simple as navigating Google Scholar, learning how social scientists locate scholarly literature and gather evidence. By the end of the program, students were presenting their independent research projects and manuscripts!

My students were the absolute best! I am incredibly thankful to the undergraduate students, graduate students, and professors who volunteered their time to lead panels and workshops. The future is bright with them!
